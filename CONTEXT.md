# MoveLink

The language of MoveLink, the **Tool** on `swavely.lol` (at `/move`) for rounding up
friends to help someone move. People, sign-in, **Joined** and the Shelf belong to the Hub
(`../swavely-lol/CONTEXT.md`). This file is a glossary, not a spec.

## Language

**Move**:
One household's moving day, or a weekend of them, at one or more **Places**. Set up by
an **Organizer** and shared with friends by its **Move link**. Has one or more
**Shifts** and, usually, some **Needs** and **Pitch-ins**. Forgotten, Helpers and all,
30 days after its last Shift (ADR-0001).
_Avoid_: Event, job, project, party

**Organizer**:
A Person who sets up and runs a **Move**: its Places, Shifts, Needs and Pitch-ins, who
has signed up, fixing or removing a **Helper's** answers. Not always the one moving
(Nathan runs his mom's Move). Any Person may start a Move and is its first Organizer; an
Organizer may add others, adding them as People if they are not yet. All of a Move's
Organizers are equal, and a Move always has at least one. The same word, and the same
idea, as the gift exchange's.
_Avoid_: Mover (that is who you hire), host, owner, admin

**Helper**:
Someone who signs up from the **Move link**: a name, no sign-in, and if they like a
phone number, which only the Organizers see. A Helper signs up for **Shifts**, takes
**Needs** and adds to **Pitch-ins**; anyone with the link sees all three. A Helper may
make themselves a Person (the Hub's **Joined**) while the Move is still to come, and then
reach their answers from any device; what they said comes with them. Someone who is
already a Person, signed in, signs up as themselves and types no name.
_Avoid_: Mover, volunteer, guest, Participant (the gift exchange's), Claim (the gift
exchange's is hidden from its owner; taking a Need never is)

**Move link**:
The one unguessable address of a **Move**, dropped in a group text. Whoever holds it can
see all of the Move, addresses and who has signed up included, and sign up without
signing in; a browser that signed up is remembered, so it can change or cancel what it
said. Forwarding it lets someone in; that is accepted, and an Organizer who finds it
somewhere it should not be replaces it. The old link then only says it was replaced;
everyone who signed up keeps what they said.
_Avoid_: Invite, invitation (the Hub's Invite-only is about People), share code

**Place**:
Somewhere a **Move** happens: an address, a label ("old house", "storage", "new
apartment") and a note for parking, door codes or stairs. A Move has one or more; each
**Shift** happens at exactly one.
_Avoid_: Location, address (a Place has one), stop, from/to

**Shift**:
A block of time at one **Place** in a **Move**, with a job and how many people it needs:
"Load at the old house, Sat 9–12, 4 people." The number is a goal, not a cap: a Shift
that has its four still takes more. A simple Move has exactly one. Helpers say yes to
Shifts, not to the Move as a whole.
_Avoid_: Slot, session, time, block

**Need**:
Something a **Move** needs besides people that one **Helper** covers: a pickup truck, a
dolly, pizza for the crew. Once taken it is theirs, so two do not turn up with the same
thing; two trucks are two Needs. A Helper may offer one nobody listed ("I have a dolly"),
which is then a Need they have taken.
_Avoid_: Item (the gift exchange's word), task, request, supply

**Pitch-in**:
Something a **Move** wants more of than one Helper brings: boxes, tape, drinks, moving
blankets. Many Helpers each add some ("about 10 boxes"), towards a rough goal if the
Organizer sets one, which, like a **Shift's**, is not a cap. The test against a **Need**:
would two people bringing it be a problem? A truck, yes; boxes, no.
_Avoid_: Supply, collection, shared need, Need (which has one taker)
